## PhD Students and Masters

We value intellectual curiosity and a positive, open-minded attitude toward research. We place much more emphasis on **going deep enough on each project** than on publishing many papers. We expect each of your new paper to be a clear step forward from the previous one—in depth, rigor, insight, or overall quality.

We aim to run the group in a student-centered way, helping you gradually take ownership of your research and develop your own projects within our shared interests. We encourage candid discussion: please do not hesitate to disagree with us or question our assumptions. I also continue to reflect on my advising and strive to become a better mentor over time.

## Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate research is an opportunity to explore your interests and learn how to investigate meaningful questions. We value genuine curiosity, a willingness to learn, and thoughtful engagement more than prior research experience. Before contacting us, please read **five of our recent papers** and tell us in your email **which one or two you like most and why**. Please also attach your **CV and transcript**.

We hope you will engage deeply with a project. Publications can be valuable outcomes, but your learning and growth matter more. Please approach research in the group as an important required course: consider your overall workload before joining and set aside regular time you can sustain throughout the semester. We also hope you will **work with only one research group at a time**, giving your project the focus and continuity it needs. Questions and differing opinions are always welcome, and if your availability changes, please let us know early.

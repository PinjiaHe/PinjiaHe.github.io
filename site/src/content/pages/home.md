I am an Associate Professor at the School of Data Science and the School of Artificial Intelligence, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I also serve as Assistant Dean (Research) at the School of Data Science.

My work has been published at top venues such as ICSE, FSE, ASE, ISSTA, ICML, NeurIPS, and ICLR, and has been cited [10,000+](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vg0moI0AAAAJ&hl=en) times according to Google Scholar. I received the [IEEE CS TCSE Rising Star Award](https://tc.computer.org/tcse/awards/) in 2025 (the sole recipient worldwide that year). My work has received [7,000+](https://github.com/logpai) GitHub stars and [100,000+](https://zenodo.org/records/3227177) downloads from [450+](https://github.com/logpai/loghub/wiki/Loghub-download-list) organizations, and has been well recognized by industry, including Anthropic, Microsoft, Tencent, and ByteDance.

Before joining CUHK-Shenzhen, I was a postdoctoral scholar at ETH Zurich, mentored by [Zhendong Su](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~su/). I received my Ph.D. from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by [Michael R. Lyu](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/lyu/), and my B.E. from South China University of Technology.

My primary research interests lie at the intersection of software engineering and AI. I am also interested in CS and AI education.

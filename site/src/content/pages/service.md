I serve as an *Associate Editor* of ACM [TOSEM](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tosem) and *Schedule Chair* for [FSE 2027](https://conf.researchr.org/committee/fse-2027/fse-2027-organizing-committee), and served as *Social Media Co-Chair* for [FSE 2025](https://conf.researchr.org/committee/fse-2025/fse-2025-organizing-committee).

I co-organize [EXPRESS 2026](https://conf.researchr.org/home/splash-issta-2026/express-2026), the 2nd Workshop on Explainable and Reliable Software Systems.

My program and review committee service mainly includes ICSE, FSE, ASE, ISSTA, and OOPSLA.
